\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In this paper we asked whether the dataset affects the robustness of weight-only HQQ quantization, and how.
Bit width and group size set the level of \term{accuracy retention}, and in the transition band the five UFG cultivar datasets lose more than the FG datasets in every setting, a separation that is stable across backbones and training strategies.
The \term{vulnerable-tail margin} of the baseline logits correlates with the difficulty, and matching the margin distributions narrows the \term{retention gap} by a median of \V{reductionMedian}\% without closing it.
Since most UFG swaps go beyond the baseline's second choice, margins to lower-ranked classes are the next step.
Each dataset is tied to its trained checkpoints, with one checkpoint and one inference seed per context, so these differences are associations rather than effects of the data alone.
